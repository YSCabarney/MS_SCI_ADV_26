Motion Evidence Cards

Homework: build a Word Wall for your workspace at school using double-sided index cards — one vocabulary word per card, built from today's Motion Files.

📇 17 terms 🧩 12 core + 5 bonus 🧵 Post on your wall

How to Build a Card

  1. Grab two blank index cards for every vocabulary word.
  2. On Card 1, write just the vocabulary word — big, bold, easy to read from across the room. This is the FRONT.
  3. On Card 2, write the definition in your own words (not copied straight from the notes) — this is the BACK.
  4. Glue or tape the two cards together back-to-back (blank sides facing each other), so the word shows when you look at one side, and the definition shows when you flip it over.
  5. Post your finished cards on the wall of your workspace at school — that's your Word Wall!
MATERIALS: 34 index cards (2 per word × 17 words) · glue stick or tape · markers · optional: string/tape for hanging

Using Your Word Wall

Once your cards are posted...

  • Cover the definition side and try to say it from memory — check yourself by flipping the card.
  • Pick one card a day and use the word correctly in a sentence about sports, school, or everyday life.
  • Quiz a partner over Zoom: hold up a card and see if she can define it before you flip it.
  • Sort your cards into pairs that go together (like Speed / Velocity or Scalar / Vector) and explain why they're connected.
