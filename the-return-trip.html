Motion Investigation Bureau — Case 06 · Partner Lab

The Return Trip

Strap a GPS tracker onto a runner. You and your partner control her speed on each leg of a route — then read the tracker's own numbers to find out whether "how fast" and "how fast, which way" ever agree.

Partners: 2 per breakout room Time: 15–20 min Prereq: Case 01–02, The Motion Files
How This Case Works
  1. Both partners look at the same mission tab below — read the target out loud to each other.
  2. Write your prediction before touching any sliders. Guessing wrong is fine; that's the point.
  3. Drag the Leg 1–3 sliders to design a route. The tracker updates live — watch the pill flip to Mission Solved.
  4. Hit Run Simulation to watch it play out, then write your Case Note together. One partner types; both talk it through.

Bring This Back to the Bureau

  1. Share your mission and the exact slider values that solved it.
  2. Was there a moment your speed and velocity readouts disagreed the most? What was the runner doing on the track at that instant?
  3. Case 01 claimed "slope of a position-time graph = speed." After today, how would you correct that claim now that direction is in the picture?

Every route above is legal — a real GPS tracker on a real athlete would report the exact same distance, displacement, speed, and velocity numbers you just saw.
