Machine Lab

Pick a machine, pick a load, and find out how much force YOU need to move it, and how far you'll have to push or pull. ← Back to unit page

How to use it

  1. Choose a machine tab: Ramp, Lever, or Pulley.
  2. Choose a load and move the sliders.
  3. Click ▶ Move it! to watch the load move. Compare how far the load moves with how far your effort moves.
  4. Click ＋ Record trial to save the numbers in the data table. Copy them onto your worksheet.

The math behind the lab

Work = force × distance  (joules, J)
Work out = load × load distance  ·  Work in = effort × effort distance
Mechanical advantage (MA) = load ÷ effort
Efficiency = work out ÷ work in × 100%

This is a simplified model built for class. Weights use 1 kg ≈ 10 N. Friction settings are rough classroom values: the "wet sand" setting is based on a 2014 University of Amsterdam experiment that found damp sand cut the pulling force on a sled about in half.
