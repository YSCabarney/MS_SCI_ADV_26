Words you'll need today

FrictionA force that rubs against something moving and slows it down.
Speed trapA section of track with a tape mark at each end. You time the ball between the marks to find its speed.
TrialOne run of the experiment. You'll do 3 trials on each surface.
Stopping distanceHow far the ball rolls on the surface before it stops.

Warm-up C · ForcesWhile the ball rolls on the carpet, are the forces balanced or unbalanced?
Show answer

Unbalanced. The ball is slowing down, so its motion is changing. Friction pulls backward, and nothing pushes forward.

Warm-up D · InertiaNothing pushes the ball after it leaves the track. So why does it keep rolling?
Show answer

Inertia. A moving object keeps moving until a force stops it. Here, that force is friction.

Step 3

Get Your Materials

🧵 2 half-pipes (from your Coaster Lab kit)⚪ The same ball from the Coaster Lab 📚 A stack of books🔵 Painter's tape📏 Tape measure ⏱️ Phone stopwatch

Pick 3 surfaces

Choose 3 from this list. Pick at least 1 hard and 1 soft.

Soft: bath towel · rug or carpet · yoga mat · fleece blanket
Hard: tile or wood floor

Lay each one flat, with no wrinkles. Your partner must pick at least 1 of the same surfaces as you, so you can compare.

If the ball rolls more than 3 m on the hard floor, or hits a wall, pick a different soft surface instead.

Safety: roll the steel ball only on the floor, and never throw it. Keep it away from little kids and pets. Pick up the ball after every trial so nobody steps on it.

Step 4

Build the Ramp

5 min
  1. Lean half-pipe #1 on a stack of books. Make the top 20 cm high, measured straight up from the floor. Tape it so it can't slide.
  2. Lay half-pipe #2 flat on the floor at the bottom of the ramp. Tape the two pieces together so there's no bump.
  3. On the flat piece, put a piece of tape for Mark 1. Measure 60 cm and put another piece of tape for Mark 2. Mark 2 should be at the very end of the track.
  4. Lay your first surface flat on the floor, touching the end of the track.
  5. Test it: set the ball at the top and let go. Don't push it.
The ball rolls down, through both marks, off the end of the track, and onto the surface. If it gets stuck or jumps out, check the tape at the joint.
Step 5

Practice the Timer

2 min

You'll use the Lap button on your phone's stopwatch. It lets you get 2 times from 1 roll. Tap 3 times:

Tap 1 · StartWhen the ball reaches Mark 1
Tap 2 · LapWhen the ball reaches Mark 2 (the end of the track)
Tap 3 · StopThe moment the ball stops rolling

Your phone will then show something like this:

Lap 10.48 s← trap time (how long through the speed trap)Lap 21.40 s← stopping time (how long until it stopped)

Practice 2 or 3 times before you write anything down.

Three taps too hard? Roll the ball twice: time the speed trap on the first roll, then time the stop on the second roll. Write that in your report.

Step 6

Roll and Record

≈ 18 min
  1. Set the ball at the top of the ramp. Let go. Don't push it.
  2. Use the 3 taps to time it.
  3. Measure the stopping distance in cm, from Mark 2 to where the ball stopped.
  4. Write down 3 numbers: trap time, stopping time, stopping distance.
  5. Do this 3 times on the same surface. That's 3 trials.
  6. Switch to your next surface and repeat. Do all 3 surfaces.

Step 7

Do the Math

≈ 7 min

Do these 4 steps for each surface. The gray boxes show an example with practice numbers.

A

Find the average trap time

Add your 3 trap times, then divide by 3.

(0.48 + 0.52 + 0.50) ÷ 3 = 0.50 s
B

Find the starting speed

speed = distance ÷ time. The distance is always 0.60 m (the speed trap).

0.60 ÷ 0.50 = 1.2 m/s
C

Find the average stopping time

Add your 3 stopping times, then divide by 3.

(1.4 + 1.6 + 1.5) ÷ 3 = 1.5 s
D

Find the acceleration

a = (final − starting) ÷ time. Final is 0 because the ball stopped. Starting is your speed from Step B.

(0 − 1.2) ÷ 1.5 = −0.8 m/s²

Check yourself: every acceleration should be a negative number, because the ball is slowing down. Got a positive number? You probably did (1.2 − 0) instead of (0 − 1.2). Always do final minus starting.
