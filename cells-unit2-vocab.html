10 new words to know. Make your index cards using your art supplies — front: word, back: definition in YOUR words.

🎨 How to Make Your Index Cards

1
Write the word on the front of your index card.
2
Write the definition in your own words on the back.
3
Add a sketch or doodle that helps YOU remember it.
4
Color-code it! New words = purple, review words = blue.
5
Post it on your word wall (or keep it in your vocab set).

You'll need:

📇 Index cards (10 new + 12 review) ✏️ Pencil 🖊️ Markers or pens 🎨 Colored pencils 📐 Ruler (optional)
