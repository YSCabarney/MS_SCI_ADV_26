Cell Vocab Word Wall

Turn tonight's vocabulary into an index-card word wall for your practice space at school — front side is the word, back side is the definition.

12 terms Due next practice/class Post on your word wall

What you're making: For each vocabulary word below, glue or tape two index cards together back-to-back. Write the word on the front card and the definition (in your own words) on the back card. Once they're done, post them on the wall of your word space at school so your whole team can study them.

What You'll Need

  1. Materials: 24 index cards (2 per word), glue stick or tape, markers/pens, optional colored pencils for a small doodle.
  2. Step 1: Write the vocab word big and bold on the front card.
  3. Step 2: Write the definition in your own words on the back card — don't just copy it exactly.
  4. Step 3: Glue the two cards back-to-back so the word and definition line up on one card.
  5. Step 4: Optional: add a tiny sketch or your own real-world example to help it stick in your memory.
  6. Step 5: Post all 12 finished cards on your word wall space.
