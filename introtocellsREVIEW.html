Round 3 · Led by Fact Checker

Coach's Claim

Read each claim out loud. Team calls "Confirmed!" (true) or "Busted!" (false) before Fact Checker clicks an answer.

Claim 1 All living things are made of at least one cell.
✅ Confirmed — postulate #1 of cell theory.
Claim 2 If the conditions are right, a brand-new cell can appear out of nowhere with no parent cell.
❌ Busted — Virchow's rule: all cells come from pre-existing cells.
Claim 3 A mature red blood cell has a nucleus, just like almost every other cell in your body.
❌ Busted — mature red blood cells lose their nucleus to carry more oxygen.
Claim 4 Scientists are still discovering brand-new organelles today.
✅ Confirmed — the "hemifusome" was identified in 2024–2025.
Claim 5 The mitochondria's job is to package and ship proteins out of the cell.
❌ Busted — that's the Golgi apparatus's job. Mitochondria make energy.
