Part 2

Cell Science in the News

Mission: Cell theory was finished in 1855 — but scientists are still discovering new things about cells right now, the same way you learned in class about the newly discovered "hemifusome." Find ONE real, current news article, video, or clip about cells, genetics, medicine, or biology.

🔍 Try searching: "gene therapy" 🔍 "stem cells" 🔍 "cancer research" 🔍 "new organelle" 🔍 "CRISPR"
  1. Summarize it: in 2–3 sentences, what is the article/video actually about?
  2. Connect it: in 1–2 sentences, link it to ONE idea from class — a cell theory postulate or a specific organelle.
  3. Cite it: include the title and link (or where you found it).
Final Reflection — 1 sentence

Why does it matter that scientists are still discovering new things about cells, even though cell theory itself hasn't changed?
