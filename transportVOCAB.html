Cell Transport Word Wall

Ten new words for how materials get into and out of a cell, and each one comes with an animation that shows it in action. Add them to your word wall next to your Cell Membrane card.

10 terms Due next class Post on your word wall

Same routine as last time, plus one new step. For each word, glue two index cards back-to-back. Write the word on the front and the definition in your own words on the back. New: watch each word's animation, then add a tiny sketch with arrows on the back that shows what you saw.

What You'll Need

  1. Materials: 20 index cards (2 per word), glue stick or tape, markers.
  2. Front: write the word big and bold. Use teal/green for passive words and orange/red for active words. Use black for words that describe the membrane or the setup.
  3. Back: the definition in your own words, plus a mini sketch from the animation.
  4. Post all 10 on your word wall and bring them to class for the flash round.

How to Read the Animations

membrane (two rows of beads) small molecule (oxygen) water large molecule or salt sugar or protein ⚡ energy from mitochondria

The shaded side is inside the cell. Use ❚❚ Pause to freeze a moment for your sketch, and ↺ Replay to watch from the start.

Your 10 Words

01

Selectively Permeable

Membrane

Describes a membrane that lets some materials pass through but blocks others.

Real worldA gym door with a check-in table: teammates get in, but not everyone does.

02

Concentration Gradient

Setup

A difference in how crowded a material is between two areas, going from high concentration to low.

Real worldA full ball cart next to an empty court. The bigger the difference, the steeper the "hill."

03

Equilibrium

Setup

When a material is evenly spread out on both sides. The particles keep moving, but there's no overall change.

Real worldRound 1: once the balls were even on both sides, they still crossed the line both ways.

04

Passive Transport

Passive

Movement of materials across the cell membrane from high to low concentration, with no energy used by the cell.

Real worldA ball rolling downhill. Nobody has to push it.

05

Diffusion

Passive

Movement of particles from an area of high concentration to an area of low concentration.

Real worldPopcorn smell spreading through the house, or food coloring spreading through a glass of water.

06

Osmosis

Passive

The diffusion of water across a selectively permeable membrane.

Real worldWilted lettuce crisping up in cold water, or a salted cucumber "sweating."

07

Active Transport

Active

Movement of materials across the cell membrane from low to high concentration, using energy from the cell.

Real worldRound 2: shagging every ball back into the crowded corner.

08

Transport Protein

Passive or Active

A protein in the cell membrane that works as a door or a pump to move materials across.

Real worldA turnstile at a stadium: only people with the right ticket get through.

09

Endocytosis

Active

When the cell membrane folds around a large particle and brings it into the cell in a bubble called a vesicle.

Real worldA white blood cell swallowing a germ.

10

Exocytosis

Active

When a vesicle joins with the cell membrane and releases its contents outside the cell.

Real worldThe Golgi's "packages" being shipped out the door.
